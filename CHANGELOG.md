# Changelog

Newest first. A version is **released** when its tag (e.g. `v0.2.0`) is pushed to GitHub. The Quartermaster checks
GitHub every 10 minutes and posts each newly released entry to #patch-notes, one message per version, so the channel
keeps the full history. Never edit a released entry; add a new one.

Format: `## vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH · YYYY-MM-DD · Short title`, then bullet points written for members.

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org):
- **MAJOR** (v1.0.0 → v2.0.0): breaking for members: progress reset, ranks or XP rebalanced, commands removed or renamed.
- **MINOR** (v1.2.0 → v1.3.0): new things that don't break anything: quests, channels, commands, features.
- **PATCH** (v1.2.3 → v1.2.4): fixes only: typos, links, bugs, wording.
- While MAJOR is **0**, Unrealcraft is pre-release and anything may still change between MINOR versions.
  **v1.0.0** marks the official public launch.

## v0.1.0 · 2026-09-29 · First release
- **Welcome to Unrealcraft**, a Discord RPG for learning Unreal Engine 5. You rank up by finishing quests (read the official docs, do the work in Unreal, pass a quiz, turn it in), never by chatting.
- **Getting in:** accept the rules, answer a few questions about what you want to learn, then press **Start Orientation** in #welcome. Your answers shape your path, your code taster, and whether you can test out of the Starter Quests.
- **Quests:** Orientation, the 11 **Starter Quests** everyone does, then your major's path. Level Design is fully written through Rank 3; Programming and Lookdev are in progress. Use `/quest`, `/quiz`, `/submit`, `/rank` and `/path`.
- **Checklists tick themselves** when the Quartermaster sees you do something. Screenshots and links are checked when you submit.
- **Ranks:** Greenlit → Blockout Artist → Gameplay Prototyper → Specialist → Engineer → Systems Architect → Studio Lead. At Rank 3 you pick a **Specialty** (Design, Lookdev, Anim or Code) for your title.
- **Channels:** GATE (the essentials), Guild Hub (chat, showcase, help desk, suggestions), Voice Rooms, and the Workshop (quest board plus one forum per track, each opening at its rank). **New post** in any Workshop forum opens a short form, and your turn-ins appear there automatically.
- **Help desk:** **🛠️ Unreal help** for editor and quest questions, **🐞 Server / bot problem** if something here breaks.
- **Patch notes:** every update will be written up in this channel.
